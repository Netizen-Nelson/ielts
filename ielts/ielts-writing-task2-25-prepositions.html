雅思寫作必備介詞片語：詞彙表不收、哪題都用得上的 25 個介詞片語

介詞片語是 Task 2 寫作中最被低估的得分工具。它們不修飾名詞、不描述動作，而是決定句子的邏輯關係、論述立場與分析深度。正確使用介詞片語，能讓句子從「陳述事實」進化為「精準論證」。

💡 介詞片語的作用是在主幹句之外再添一層「分析框架」。同樣一個論點，加上 in light of、on the grounds that 或 at the expense of，考官感受到的論述層次截然不同。
代價與取捨 背景、時機與情境 理由、根據與前提 目的與利益 比例、配合與一致 核心與前沿 因果、回應與面對 對比與釐清 總整理

🔴 描述代價、取捨與替代

at the expense of 以…為代價

強調在獲得某種利益的同時，某個同等重要的事物遭到犧牲，是論述「取捨困境」最精準的片語，語氣帶有隱性批判。

句型 X is achieved / pursued at the expense of Y
常見搭配 at the expense of the environment at the expense of individual wellbeing at the expense of long-term sustainability
話題例句
環境
For decades, economic development has been pursued at the expense of environmental sustainability, a trade-off that is now generating irreversible consequences for the planet.
幾十年來，經濟發展一直是以犧牲環境永續性為代價來追求的，而這種取捨如今正為地球帶來不可逆的後果。
教育
Schools that prioritise examination results at the expense of emotional wellbeing risk producing graduates who are academically competent but psychologically unprepared for adult life.
以犧牲情感健康為代價來優先追求考試成績的學校，可能培育出學術能力強、但心理上未為成人生活做好準備的畢業生。
in lieu of 代替、取而代之

比 instead of 更正式，強調以一種替代方案取代原本的做法，常用於政策討論或評估改革措施的可行性。

句型 X is adopted / offered in lieu of Y
常見搭配 in lieu of direct subsidies in lieu of traditional examinations in lieu of punitive measures
話題例句
政策
Some governments have proposed offering tax incentives in lieu of direct subsidies as a more fiscally efficient means of encouraging corporate investment in green technology.
部分政府提議以稅收優惠取代直接補貼，作為鼓勵企業投資綠色科技更具財政效率的手段。
教育
Competency-based assessments are increasingly being adopted in lieu of traditional written examinations in professional training programmes, reflecting a shift towards practical skill evaluation.
在專業培訓計畫中，能力本位評估正日益被採用以取代傳統的筆試，反映出向實務技能評估的轉變。
at the expense of vs. in lieu of：at the expense of 強調「獲得 A 但犧牲了 B」，語氣帶有批判；in lieu of 只是中性地表示「用 A 替代 B」，不帶評判意涵。

🟣 描述背景、時機與情境

in the context of 在…的背景下、在…的框架內

引入論述的大背景或分析框架，讓讀者理解某問題應在哪個維度下理解，是 Task 2 開場句和過渡句的萬用片語。

句型 In the context of X, Y must be understood as / considered...
常見搭配 in the context of globalisation in the context of rapid technological change in the broader context of
話題例句
科技
In the context of rapid digitalisation, governments face mounting pressure to update legislation that was designed for a pre-internet era and is now fundamentally ill-suited to modern realities.
在快速數位化的背景下，各國政府面臨越來越大的壓力，需要更新為網路時代之前設計、如今從根本上已不適應現代現實的立法。
全球
The debate over national sovereignty must be understood in the context of growing economic interdependence among nations, which has fundamentally altered the meaning of independent governance.
關於國家主權的辯論，必須在國家之間日益增長的經濟相互依存的背景下加以理解，這從根本上改變了獨立治理的意涵。
in the wake of 在…之後、在…的衝擊後緊接著

強調某事件之後緊接著發生的後續反應或變化，比 after 更強調「衝擊後的餘波」，常用於描述政策或社會轉變的觸發點。

句型 In the wake of X, Y has / have [changed / emerged / collapsed]
常見搭配 in the wake of the financial crisis in the wake of the pandemic in the wake of public outcry
話題例句
社會
In the wake of the financial crisis, public trust in banking institutions collapsed to historically low levels, prompting widespread calls for greater regulatory oversight.
在金融危機之後，民眾對銀行機構的信任跌至歷史最低水準，引發了對加強監管監督的廣泛呼聲。
科技
In the wake of several high-profile data breaches, regulators around the world were compelled to introduce far stricter data protection frameworks.
在幾起備受矚目的資料外洩事件之後，全球各地的監管機構被迫引入更嚴格的資料保護框架。
in the absence of 在缺乏…的情況下

強調某關鍵條件不存在時所帶來的後果或困境，是論述「為何問題難以解決」或「為何需要某政策」的核心片語。

句型 In the absence of X, Y is/are [unable to / forced to / unlikely to]
常見搭配 in the absence of regulation in the absence of political will in the absence of evidence
話題例句
政策
In the absence of robust international agreements, individual nations have little structural incentive to reduce carbon emissions unilaterally at the cost of their own economic competitiveness.
在缺乏有力的國際協議的情況下，各個國家幾乎沒有結構性動力，以犧牲自身經濟競爭力為代價單方面減少碳排放。
教育
In the absence of qualified teachers, many schools in rural areas have turned to digital platforms as a substitute, with mixed results in terms of student engagement and outcomes.
在缺乏合格教師的情況下，許多農村地區的學校已轉向數位平台作為替代，在學生參與度和學習成果方面成效參差不齊。
in the event of 萬一…發生時、在…情況下

用來討論假設性或應急性情境，強調預防性準備的必要，常出現在論述政府責任或政策規劃的段落。

句型 In the event of X, governments / institutions must [have / ensure / be prepared to]
常見搭配 in the event of a public health emergency in the event of economic recession in the event of system failure
話題例句
政策
In the event of a public health emergency, governments must have pre-established protocols that enable rapid, coordinated responses without being impeded by bureaucratic delays.
萬一發生公共衛生緊急情況，政府必須擁有預先制定的協議，以便在不受官僚程序拖延的情況下快速、協調地應對。
環境
Coastal cities must develop detailed contingency plans in the event of sea level rises exceeding current projected models, as the consequences of unpreparedness could prove catastrophic.
沿海城市必須制定詳細的應急計畫，以應對萬一海平面上升超過當前預測模型的情況，因為準備不足的後果可能是災難性的。

🟡 描述理由、根據與前提

in light of 鑒於、考慮到

引入支持某立場的新證據或情況，語氣比 considering 更正式，暗示「在這些事實面前，某結論是合理的」，常用於結論段或政策建議段。

句型 In light of X, it is [clear / necessary / arguable] that Y
常見搭配 in light of recent evidence in light of these findings in light of growing concerns about
話題例句
健康
In light of growing evidence linking ultra-processed foods to chronic disease, many health authorities are now calling for mandatory nutritional labelling on all packaged food products.
鑒於越來越多將超加工食品與慢性病聯繫起來的證據，許多衛生機構現在正呼籲對所有包裝食品實施強制性營養標籤。
科技
In light of recent advances in renewable energy technology, the economic case for continued investment in fossil fuel infrastructure has become increasingly difficult to justify.
鑒於可再生能源技術的最新進展，繼續投資化石燃料基礎設施的經濟論據已愈發難以站穩腳跟。
on the grounds that / of 以…為理由、基於…的立場

明確說明某行動或立場所依據的理由，比 because 更正式，帶有「公開聲稱的依據」語感，常與 justify 或 oppose 搭配。

句型 X is justified / opposed / rejected on the grounds that / of Y
常見搭配 justified on the grounds that dismissed on the grounds of cost defended on the grounds of national security
話題例句
政策
Several governments have justified the expansion of surveillance powers on the grounds that national security concerns outweigh individual privacy rights in an era of heightened global threat.
多個政府以在全球威脅加劇的時代，國家安全考量重於個人隱私權為由，為擴大監控權力的做法進行辯護。
教育
Many educators oppose the widespread use of standardised testing on the grounds that it fails to capture the full range of a student's abilities and systematically disadvantages creative learners.
許多教育工作者以標準化考試未能反映學生全面能力、且系統性地使有創意的學習者處於不利地位為由，反對其廣泛應用。
on the premise that 以…為前提、基於…的假設

強調某論點或政策建立在一個尚待驗證或值得質疑的前提之上，是反駁論點的利器，也可用於揭示某政策的根本假設。

句型 X is built / designed / argued on the premise that Y
常見搭配 built on the premise that operate on the false premise that rest on the premise that
話題例句
經濟
Many free-market policy recommendations are designed on the premise that market competition will naturally self-regulate over time, an assumption that decades of financial crises have repeatedly called into question.
許多自由市場政策建議是以市場競爭從長遠來看會自然自我調節為前提設計的，而這一假設已被數十年的金融危機反覆質疑。
教育
The traditional education system was largely constructed on the premise that academic excellence is a reliable predictor of professional success, a claim that has been increasingly challenged by research on emotional intelligence and practical competence.
傳統教育體系在很大程度上是以學術優秀是職業成功可靠預測指標為前提建構的，而這一說法已受到情商和實務能力研究的日益挑戰。
by virtue of 憑藉、由於

強調某優勢或特質是由某固有條件帶來的，比 because of 更正式，帶有「本質上即如此」的語感，常用於分析結構性不平等。

句型 X [holds an advantage / is exposed to risk] by virtue of Y
常見搭配 by virtue of their position by virtue of access to by virtue of their size
話題例句
環境
By virtue of their geographical location, low-lying coastal communities are disproportionately exposed to the risks associated with rising sea levels, despite having contributed the least to global carbon emissions.
憑藉其地理位置，低窪沿海社群不成比例地暴露在與海平面上升相關的風險中，儘管他們對全球碳排放的貢獻最少。
科技
By virtue of their access to vast proprietary datasets, major technology companies hold a structural competitive advantage that smaller rivals are fundamentally unable to replicate.
憑藉對龐大專有數據集的取得，主要科技公司擁有結構性競爭優勢，較小的競爭對手從根本上無法複製。
on the basis of 基於、根據

說明某決定、行動或評估所依據的標準或依據，比 based on 更正式，常出現在批評歧視或論述政策合理性的段落。

句型 Decisions made / people discriminated against on the basis of X
常見搭配 on the basis of merit discriminated against on the basis of on the basis of short-term indicators
話題例句
社會
Discriminating against individuals on the basis of their ethnic background, gender, or religious beliefs represents a fundamental violation of internationally recognised human rights principles.
基於個人的族裔背景、性別或宗教信仰對其進行歧視，是對國際公認人權原則的根本性侵犯。
政策
Policy decisions made solely on the basis of short-term economic indicators risk overlooking the long-term social and environmental costs that will ultimately fall on future generations.
僅僅基於短期經濟指標做出的政策決定，可能忽視最終將落在後代身上的長期社會和環境代價。
in view of 鑒於、考量整體情況

廣泛考量某整體處境或背景後得出結論，比 in light of 更強調「綜觀全局」，而非聚焦於某一條具體新證據，語氣稍帶不得不然的無奈感。

句型 In view of X, it is [no longer tenable / unreasonable / necessary] to...
常見搭配 in view of the current situation in view of these circumstances in view of the scale of the problem
話題例句
環境
In view of the accelerating pace of climate change, maintaining a business-as-usual approach to industrial production is no longer a viable or ethically defensible position for any responsible government.
鑒於氣候變遷加速的步伐，繼續維持工業生產一切照舊的做法，已不再是任何負責任的政府可行或在倫理上站得住腳的立場。
社會
In view of the widening gap between housing costs and average wages, governments cannot reasonably expect market forces alone to resolve the urban affordability crisis without direct intervention.
鑒於住房成本與平均工資之間日益擴大的差距，政府不能合理地期望僅靠市場力量、不經直接干預，就能解決城市住房負擔危機。
in view of vs. in light of：in light of 強調「有了這條新證據，所以得出某結論」，指向具體的觸發資訊；in view of 強調「考量整體處境之後」，視野更廣，語氣帶有無可迴避的現實感。兩者可互換，但 in light of 更常搭配研究或數據，in view of 更常搭配整體局勢。

🟢 描述目的與利益

with a view to 旨在、以…為目的

強調某行動是為了達成某目標而採取的，比 in order to 更書面，常接動名詞（V-ing），多用於政策、改革或計畫的論述。

句型 X is implemented / restructured with a view to [V-ing]
常見搭配 with a view to reducing inequality with a view to achieving long-term sustainability with a view to improving outcomes
話題例句
教育
Many governments are restructuring their education systems with a view to better equipping graduates for the demands of a rapidly evolving labour market that increasingly values adaptability over rote knowledge.
許多政府正在重組其教育體系，旨在更好地使畢業生為快速演變的勞動力市場的需求做好準備，該市場日益重視適應能力而非死記硬背的知識。
政策
International climate summits are convened with a view to establishing legally binding agreements that hold all participating nations equally accountable for meeting their stated emissions reduction commitments.
國際氣候峰會的召開旨在建立具有法律約束力的協議，使所有參與國對履行其既定的減排承諾平等負責。
in the interests of 為了…的利益、以…為出發點

表明某行動是為了某群體或某更大目標的利益，常用於論述政府責任或批判某政策只服務特定利益，語氣帶有道德訴求。

句型 In the interests of X, governments / institutions must / should...
常見搭配 in the interests of public health in the interests of social cohesion in the interests of future generations
話題例句
健康
In the interests of long-term public health, governments have a clear responsibility to regulate the marketing of products that are demonstrably harmful, regardless of the commercial interests at stake.
為了長期公共健康的利益，政府有明確責任監管被證明有害的產品的行銷活動，無論涉及何種商業利益。
社會
In the interests of social cohesion, policymakers must ensure that the economic benefits of growth are distributed equitably across all segments of society, not concentrated among the already privileged.
為了社會凝聚力的利益，政策制定者必須確保經濟增長的好處在社會所有階層中得到公平分配，而非集中在已享有特權的群體中。
for the sake of 為了…的緣故、為了…著想

強調為了某更高目標或某群體而甘願付出或讓步，比 in the interests of 更個人化、更有情感重量，常暗示需要犧牲某眼前利益，也常用於批判某行動只是流於表面。

句型 For the sake of X, Y must [sacrifice / prioritise / act]
常見搭配 for the sake of future generations for the sake of short-term political gain for the sake of transparency
話題例句
環境
For the sake of future generations, current societies have a moral obligation to address the structural causes of climate change rather than deferring the burden of action indefinitely onto those who will inherit the consequences.
為了後代著想，當代社會有道德義務解決氣候變遷的結構性原因，而非無限期地將行動的責任推卸給將承擔後果的人。
政策
Short-term economic growth should not be relentlessly pursued for the sake of political popularity if doing so systematically undermines the environmental and social foundations on which long-term prosperity depends.
若這樣做系統性地破壞了長期繁榮所依賴的環境和社會基礎，則不應為了政治聲望的緣故不惜一切地追求短期經濟增長。
for the sake of vs. in the interests of：in the interests of 偏向客觀制度語氣，強調某群體的「最大利益」；for the sake of 更個人化、更有情感份量，常暗示需要主動犧牲或讓步，也更常出現在道德訴求或批判某行為動機的脈絡中。

🔵 描述比例、配合與一致性

in proportion to 與…成比例

強調某分配或變化應與某基準保持合理比例，常用於論述稅制公平、資源分配或政策力度的合理性。

句型 X should increase / be distributed in proportion to Y
常見搭配 in proportion to income not in proportion to the scale of the problem in proportion to the level of risk
話題例句
政策
A progressive tax system should be designed so that contributions increase in proportion to individual income, ensuring that those with greater financial capacity bear a fair share of the collective fiscal burden.
累進稅制的設計應使貢獻與個人收入成比例增加，確保財務能力較強者承擔公平份額的集體財政負擔。
環境
Carbon taxes should be levied in proportion to the volume of emissions produced by each entity, creating a direct and transparent financial incentive for companies to invest in cleaner production methods.
碳稅應與每個實體產生的排放量成比例徵收，為企業投資更清潔的生產方法創造直接透明的財務激勵。
in conjunction with 與…結合、配合…同時

強調某措施必須與其他配套措施並行才能發揮效果，是論述「單一政策不足夠」的核心片語，展現論述的全面性。

句型 X must be implemented in conjunction with Y to achieve Z
常見搭配 used in conjunction with implemented in conjunction with complementary policies deployed in conjunction with
話題例句
政策
Infrastructure investment alone is rarely sufficient to revitalise struggling economies; it must be implemented in conjunction with complementary policies on workforce training, housing, and local employment generation.
單純的基礎設施投資很少足以振興陷入困境的經濟體；它必須與勞動力培訓、住房和地方就業創造方面的配套政策結合實施。
教育
Technology is most effective as a pedagogical tool when used in conjunction with strong teacher-led instruction, rather than as a standalone replacement for face-to-face interaction.
科技作為教學工具最有效的方式是與強有力的教師主導教學結合使用，而非作為面對面互動的獨立替代品。
in line with 與…一致、符合

強調某行動或結果與某標準、目標或趨勢保持一致，可用於正面（符合預期）或反面（未能符合），是評估政策落實的重要片語。

句型 X must / has failed to increase / change in line with Y
常見搭配 in line with international standards in line with emissions targets failed to rise in line with productivity
話題例句
政策
Government spending on renewable energy must increase significantly in line with the ambitious emissions reduction targets set at recent international climate summits if these commitments are to be taken seriously.
政府在可再生能源上的支出必須大幅增加，以符合近期國際氣候峰會設定的雄心勃勃的減排目標，如此這些承諾才能被認真對待。
社會
Wages across many sectors have consistently failed to rise in line with productivity gains over the past three decades, contributing directly to the widening of economic inequality.
過去三十年來，許多行業的工資始終未能與生產力提升同步增長，直接加劇了經濟不平等的擴大。
irrespective of 不管…、無論…如何

強調某原則或政策不應因特定條件而有例外，比 regardless of 更書面，是論述普遍性原則時最有力的片語之一。

句型 X should be accessible / applied irrespective of Y
常見搭配 irrespective of socioeconomic background irrespective of political affiliation irrespective of short-term economic cost
話題例句
教育
Quality education should be accessible to all children irrespective of their socioeconomic background, geographical location, or the financial circumstances of their families.
所有兒童無論其社會經濟背景、地理位置或家庭財務狀況如何，都應能獲得優質教育。
政策
Carbon emissions must be reduced irrespective of which political party holds power, as the consequences of inaction will extend far beyond any single electoral cycle or national interest.
無論哪個政黨掌權，都必須減少碳排放，因為不作為的後果將遠遠超出任何單一選舉週期或國家利益的範疇。

⚪ 描述核心位置與最前沿

at the heart of 在…的核心、…的根本所在

指出某問題或議題的根本核心，讓論述直接切入最關鍵的矛盾點，比 the most important aspect of 更有文學力量，是開場段與分析段的利器。

句型 At the heart of [the debate / the problem / the issue] lies X
常見搭配 at the heart of the debate lies at the heart of this problem is at the heart of any effective solution must be
話題例句
社會
At the heart of the immigration debate lies a fundamental tension between the desire to preserve national identity and the recognition of the economic and cultural benefits that migration brings.
移民辯論的核心，是保護國家認同的願望與承認移民帶來的經濟和文化效益之間的根本張力。
政策
At the heart of any effective public health strategy must be a long-term commitment to addressing the social determinants of health, rather than focusing narrowly on the treatment of its symptoms.
任何有效公共衛生策略的核心，必須是對解決健康社會決定因素的長期承諾，而非僅僅聚焦於症狀的治療。
at the forefront of 處於…的最前沿、引領…

強調某國家、機構或個人在某領域居於領先位置，帶有正面語氣，常用於論述競爭優勢或政策典範。

句型 X places itself / remains at the forefront of Y
常見搭配 at the forefront of technological innovation position oneself at the forefront of remain at the forefront of
話題例句
科技
Nations that position themselves at the forefront of artificial intelligence development stand to gain significant economic and geopolitical advantages over rivals who are slower to invest in this transformative technology.
將自己置於人工智慧發展最前沿的國家，有望在經濟和地緣政治方面獲得相對於那些在這項變革性技術上投資較慢的競爭對手的顯著優勢。
環境
Scandinavian countries have consistently placed themselves at the forefront of sustainable energy policy, demonstrating that decarbonisation and economic prosperity are not mutually exclusive goals.
斯堪地那維亞國家一貫將自己置於可持續能源政策的最前沿，證明了脫碳與經濟繁榮並非相互排斥的目標。
at the heart of vs. at the forefront of：at the heart of 是往「內部核心」鑽，用來找問題的根本；at the forefront of 是往「外部前沿」看，用來描述領先地位。兩者方向相反，功能互補。

🟠 描述因果、回應與面對

in response to 回應…、針對…

描述某行動是對某外部壓力或事件的反應，比 because of 更強調「互動性」，暗示行動者是被迫或被觸發的，常帶有批判色彩。

句型 In response to X, Y [announced / revised / introduced]
常見搭配 in response to public pressure in response to mounting criticism in response to a sharp rise in
話題例句
政策
In response to mounting public pressure from environmental groups, the government announced a comprehensive review of its existing environmental protection legislation and pledged to close existing loopholes.
針對環保團體日益增大的公眾壓力，政府宣布對現有環境保護立法進行全面審查，並承諾堵住現有漏洞。
科技
Several major social media companies have revised their content moderation policies in response to sustained criticism over their failure to adequately address the spread of harmful misinformation on their platforms.
幾家主要社群媒體公司已修訂其內容審核政策，以回應外界對其未能充分應對有害錯誤資訊在其平台上傳播的持續批評。
as a consequence of 作為…的結果、由於…導致

比 because of 或 due to 更正式，強調某結果是前一個原因的直接且可預見的產物，常出現在分析問題根源或論述長期影響的段落。

句型 As a consequence of X, Y has / have [suffered / declined / emerged]
常見搭配 as a direct consequence of as an inevitable consequence of as a consequence of decades of
話題例句
環境
As a consequence of decades of unchecked industrial pollution, many rivers in densely populated regions have lost the majority of their native aquatic biodiversity, with recovery unlikely without substantial intervention.
由於數十年不受限制的工業污染所導致，許多人口密集地區的河流已失去了大部分原生水生生物多樣性，若無大規模干預，復原的可能性渺茫。
社會
As a consequence of stagnant wage growth, an increasing proportion of full-time workers now find themselves unable to afford adequate housing in major urban centres, despite holding stable employment.
由於工資增長停滯的結果，越來越多的全職工人發現，儘管擁有穩定的工作，自己仍無力在主要城市中心負擔適當的住房。
in the face of 面對…、儘管面臨…

強調在某不利條件或強大阻力下仍採取（或未採取）某行動，帶有「對抗逆境」的張力，可用於讚揚也可用於批判，是論述堅持或退縮的最佳片語。

句型 In the face of X, Y [persists / struggles / is forced to]
常見搭配 in the face of opposition in the face of economic pressure in the face of mounting evidence
話題例句
環境
Many governments have struggled to maintain consistent environmental commitments in the face of short-term economic pressures and competing electoral priorities that reward immediate growth over long-term sustainability.
許多政府難以面對短期經濟壓力和相互競爭的選舉優先事項（這些因素獎勵即時增長而非長期永續性）時，維持一致的環境承諾。
社會
In the face of rapidly rising living costs, low-income families are frequently forced into impossible choices between meeting immediate basic needs and investing in the long-term futures of their children.
面對迅速上漲的生活成本，低收入家庭往往被迫在滿足即時基本需求與投資子女長遠未來之間做出兩難選擇。

🔵 描述對比與釐清

as opposed to 而非、相對於

透過對比，精準說明某事物的本質「是什麼」，同時排除可能的混淆或錯誤認知，比 rather than 更正式，且更強調兩者之間的根本差異或優劣對比。

句型 X focuses on / should prioritise A, as opposed to B
常見搭配 as opposed to the passive absorption of as opposed to merely treating the symptoms as opposed to short-term gain
話題例句
教育
The focus of modern education should be on developing critical thinking and adaptability, as opposed to the passive absorption of pre-packaged information that traditional rote learning demands.
現代教育的重心應在於培養批判性思維和適應能力，而非傳統死記硬背所要求的被動吸收預設資訊。
政策
Governments should allocate a greater share of public health budgets to preventive measures, as opposed to concentrating resources on the costly treatment of conditions that could have been avoided through earlier intervention.
政府應將更大比例的公共衛生預算分配給預防性措施，而非將資源集中在透過早期干預本可避免的疾病的昂貴治療上。
as opposed to vs. rather than：兩者都表示對比，but rather than 更口語、更常見；as opposed to 更正式，且帶有更強烈的「兩者之間存在根本差異」的語氣，適合用來釐清論點或強調對立的優劣。在 Task 2 寫作中，as opposed to 能更清楚地標示出你的論述立場。
