【萬用開場】
物品、地點、機會、時間

1. Describe a time when you work in a group.
2. Describe a great dinner you and your friends or family members enjoyed.

兩道毫不相干的考題，運用相同的破題思維(而非僵化模板)，狠狠甩開I'm going to talk about
一、物品：用書桌上加了框的照片作為故事引子，考官會覺得這是一段真誠的個人回憶，而不是背誦出來的範本
  1. A framed photo on my desk always brings me back to a challenging group project during my sophomore year.
  2. A few photos on my Google Drive always bring me back to a hot pot dinner with my parents.
二、地點：
  1. A tiny meeting room in our university library was where our team spent several weekends on a term project.
  2. A local family-owned restaurant across from my home is where my whole family got together for a special hot pot dinner.
不限話題和主角的【萬用劇情】
－－考官從第一句就知道了——工廠模板，還是精彩故事
  • 網路盛傳的『人物題套人物模板、事件題套事件模板、地點題套地點模板』，成本風險都高
  • Nelson從官方評分標準逐字讀出來了一種萬用劇情思維，自帶陳述＋反思，大學生、上班族、剛畢業的考生，不論考題主角，人人都能說出亮點

讓你有精彩故事可說的牛肉是：

習慣獨處、靠自己的考生：從自我覺察出發——我當時怎麼想、怎麼判斷，最後做了負責任的決定。陳述是過程，反思是那個決定帶來了什麼
社交經驗多的考生：從社會覺察出發——察覺到隊友的處境、理解到不同背景立場利益(同理心)、化解一場快要爆發的衝突。故事天然有張力，反思水到渠成
兩條路，同一個終點：考官相信你真的經歷過這件事，這份相信就是給高分的底氣

看得懂示範，不等於考場說得出來。真正救急的，是把這套思維內化和活用。兩道關卡：
  1. 只練開場一句話：亂數抽十道 Part 2 題目，每題只做一件事——用物品、地點、機會、時間其中兩種，說出一個自然的開場句。不計時、不往下說，就盯著這一句，直到不假思索

思考的起點——遇到任何題目，先問自己「這件事有哪幾個面向」
Why are some cities popular with tourists?
核心論述（非完整答案）
On the cultural side, cities with a distinct historical identity — ancient temples, colonial architecture, or world-class museums — give visitors something they simply can't find at home.
On the practical side, well-connected airports, affordable accommodation, and a reputation for safety make a city easy to choose and easy to commit to.
On the experiential side, it's often the street food, the local rhythm, and the feeling of being somewhere genuinely different that keeps people coming back — and recommending the place to others.
What kinds of videos are popular in your country?
核心論述（非完整答案）
The entertainment aspect is hard to miss — short-form comedy clips and drama highlights dominate, delivering a quick emotional hit in under a minute.
The informational aspect draws a different crowd — cooking tutorials, DIY guides, and personal finance breakdowns attract viewers who want to walk away with something useful.
The lifestyle aspect might be the most telling — food vlogs and travel diaries have built loyal followings, less about learning something, more about living vicariously through someone else's day.
