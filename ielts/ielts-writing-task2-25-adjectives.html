雅思寫作必備形容詞：詞彙表不收、哪題都用得上的 25 個形容詞

這 25 個形容詞不屬於任何單一主題，卻能精準描述問題的性質、程度與複雜度，是 Task 2 高分答案中讓句子「說話有重量」的關鍵詞彙。每個詞條附上常見搭配（collocation）與兩個不同領域的中英對照例句。

💡 形容詞的作用不只是修飾名詞，更是傳遞論述立場與分析深度的訊號。同樣一句話，用 acute 和 serious，考官感受到的論述層次截然不同。
嚴重與急迫 普遍與根深 影響的深度與性質 事物的核心特質 不均等與邊緣化 複雜性與爭議 說服力與可察覺性 總整理

🔴 描述問題的嚴重程度與急迫性

acute 嚴重的、急迫的

強調問題已達到尖銳、迫切的程度，比 serious 更具緊迫感，暗示若不立即應對將產生更大傷害。

常見搭配 an acute shortage of an acute lack of an acute awareness of
話題例句
健康
The shortage of qualified mental health professionals has reached an acute level in many rural communities, leaving vulnerable populations without adequate support.
合格心理健康專業人員的短缺，在許多農村社區已達到嚴峻程度，使弱勢族群無法獲得充分的支援。
社會
Young people from low-income backgrounds face an acute lack of access to quality career guidance at the most critical stage of their development.
來自低收入背景的年輕人，在其發展最關鍵的階段，面臨嚴重缺乏優質職涯輔導的問題。
alarming 令人警覺的、令人憂慮的

強調某趨勢或數據已達到需要高度警戒的地步，常用於引出數據或統計，讓論據更具說服力。

常見搭配 an alarming rate an alarming rise in at an alarming pace
話題例句
環境
The rate at which Arctic ice is retreating has reached alarming levels, prompting urgent calls for coordinated international action.
北極冰層消退的速度已達令人警覺的程度，促使各界緊急呼籲採取協調一致的國際行動。
健康
The alarming rise in childhood obesity rates reflects the failure of public health policies to keep pace with rapidly changing dietary habits.
兒童肥胖率令人擔憂的上升，反映了公共衛生政策未能跟上快速變化飲食習慣的失敗。
unprecedented 前所未有的

強調某現象在規模或性質上超越了歷史上所有已知的例子，是 Task 2 背景段最有力的開場詞之一。

常見搭配 an unprecedented shift an unprecedented level of unprecedented disruption to
話題例句
科技
The speed at which artificial intelligence has entered the mainstream represents an unprecedented shift in the way societies process and generate knowledge.
人工智慧進入主流的速度，代表了社會處理和生成知識方式上一次前所未有的轉變。
全球
The pandemic caused unprecedented disruption to global supply chains, exposing the fragility of deeply interconnected economies.
疫情對全球供應鏈造成了前所未有的衝擊，暴露了深度相互依存的經濟體系的脆弱性。

🟣 描述現象的普遍程度與根深程度

prevalent 普遍的、盛行的

描述某現象廣泛存在於某社群或領域，比 common 更正式，帶有「已成氣候」的意涵，多用於描述問題或趨勢。

常見搭配 increasingly prevalent particularly prevalent among prevalent in many societies
話題例句
科技
Misinformation has become increasingly prevalent on social media platforms, eroding public trust in credible news sources.
錯誤資訊在社群媒體平台上日益普遍，侵蝕了民眾對可信新聞來源的信任。
健康
Sedentary behaviour is particularly prevalent among office workers who spend the majority of their day seated, contributing to a range of chronic health conditions.
久坐行為在大部分工作時間需要坐著的上班族中尤為盛行，導致了一系列慢性健康問題。
pervasive 無所不在的、深入滲透的

比 prevalent 更強烈，強調某影響已滲透至各個層面、難以逃避，帶有隱性批判語氣。

常見搭配 pervasive influence of remain pervasive in a pervasive culture of
話題例句
科技
The pervasive influence of social media algorithms on consumer behaviour raises fundamental questions about individual autonomy in the digital age.
社群媒體演算法對消費者行為無所不在的影響，引發了數位時代個人自主性的根本性問題。
社會
Gender bias remains pervasive in many professional environments, subtly shaping hiring decisions and limiting promotion prospects for women.
性別偏見在許多職業環境中仍然無所不在，悄然影響招聘決策並限制女性的晉升前景。
prevalent vs. pervasive：prevalent 是「廣泛存在」，強調數量上的普遍；pervasive 是「深度滲透」，強調影響已無所不在、難以逃脫。
entrenched 根深蒂固的、積習難改的

強調某態度、不平等或制度已深深嵌入社會結構，短期內極難改變，語氣帶有強烈的批判意味。

常見搭配 entrenched inequality entrenched attitudes deeply entrenched in
話題例句
社會
Entrenched socioeconomic inequalities cannot be resolved through short-term policy interventions alone, as they are deeply embedded in the structure of society itself.
根深蒂固的社會經濟不平等，無法單靠短期政策干預來解決，因為它們已深深嵌入社會結構本身。
政治
Entrenched partisan divisions have made meaningful legislative reform increasingly difficult to achieve in many democratic nations.
根深蒂固的黨派分歧，使許多民主國家實質性的立法改革愈發難以實現。

🟠 描述影響的深度、具體性與性質

profound 深遠的、深刻的

強調某影響觸及深層、意義重大，遠超表面，比 significant 或 great 更能展現分析深度。

常見搭配 a profound impact on have profound implications for a profound shift in
話題例句
科技
The widespread adoption of smartphones has had a profound impact on the way people form, maintain, and ultimately define their social relationships.
智慧型手機的普及對人們建立、維持並最終定義社交關係的方式產生了深遠的影響。
教育
Early childhood education can have a profound and lasting effect on an individual's cognitive development, shaping academic performance well into adulthood.
幼兒教育對個人認知發展可以產生深刻而持久的影響，塑造一直延伸至成年期的學業表現。
tangible 有形的、具體可感的

強調某成果或影響是真實可見、可量化的，而非停留在理論或承諾層面，常用於質疑政策效果。

常見搭配 tangible results tangible improvements in tangible progress on
話題例句
政策
Governments must demonstrate tangible progress on climate commitments rather than relying on vague pledges and distant long-term targets.
政府必須展示在氣候承諾上的具體進展，而非依賴模糊的承諾和遙遠的長期目標。
教育
Project-based learning has been shown to produce more tangible improvements in problem-solving skills than traditional lecture-based instruction.
專案式學習已被證明比傳統講授式教學在提升問題解決能力方面產生更具體可見的改善。
detrimental 有害的、不利的

比 harmful 或 bad 更正式，常搭配 effect 或 impact，明確指出某事物對某對象造成損害，語氣客觀而有力。

常見搭配 detrimental effect on detrimental impact on prove detrimental to
話題例句
健康
Prolonged screen exposure before bedtime has a detrimental effect on sleep quality, with particularly significant consequences for adolescents whose brains are still developing.
睡前長時間接觸螢幕對睡眠品質有有害的影響，對大腦仍在發育的青少年而言後果尤為顯著。
環境
Intensive agricultural practices have had a detrimental impact on soil quality and biodiversity across many of the world's most productive farming regions.
密集農業做法對全球許多最具生產力的農業地區的土壤品質和生物多樣性產生了不利影響。
negligible 微不足道的、可忽略的

強調某影響或差距小到幾乎可以不計，常用於反駁論點或指出政策效果不足，語氣帶有否定評估。

常見搭配 negligible impact on have a negligible effect prove negligible compared to
話題例句
政策
Critics argue that the reduction in carbon emissions achieved by the policy is negligible compared to the scale of the crisis it purports to address.
批評者認為，該政策所實現的碳排放減少量，與其聲稱要應對的危機規模相比微不足道。
科技
The environmental benefit of individual recycling efforts is negligible unless supported by systemic changes in industrial production and packaging design.
個人資源回收行為的環境效益微乎其微，除非得到工業生產和包裝設計系統性變革的支持。

⚪ 描述事物本身的核心特質

inherent 固有的、內在的

強調某特質是某事物與生俱來、不可分割的，而非外部附加的，用來指出問題的根源在結構本身而非偶發因素。

常見搭配 inherent tension between inherent limitations of inherent complexity of
話題例句
社會
There is an inherent tension between the relentless pursuit of economic growth and the urgent need to protect and restore natural ecosystems.
不懈追求經濟增長與迫切保護和恢復自然生態系統的需求之間，存在著固有的張力。
科技
The inherent complexity of artificial intelligence systems makes it extremely difficult for regulators to develop effective and adaptive oversight frameworks.
人工智慧系統固有的複雜性，使監管機構極難制定有效且具適應性的監督框架。
viable 可行的、切實可行的

強調某方案在現實條件下確實能夠運作，而非停留在理論層面，常以正反兩面出現：viable alternative 或 not viable。

常見搭配 a viable alternative to economically viable not financially viable
話題例句
環境
Solar and wind power are now economically viable alternatives to fossil fuels in most regions of the world, making the transition both feasible and affordable.
太陽能和風能如今在全球大多數地區，已成為化石燃料在經濟上可行的替代方案，使能源轉型既可實現又負擔得起。
政策
Without significant public investment, universal basic income remains a theoretically appealing but practically non-viable policy option for most governments.
若缺乏大量公共投資，全民基本收入對大多數政府而言，仍是理論上具吸引力但實際上不可行的政策選項。
sustainable 可持續的、長久維持的

不只用於環境話題，可廣泛用於描述任何長期可維持的做法、成長或政策，反義詞 unsustainable 同樣好用。

常見搭配 sustainable development not sustainable in the long term financially sustainable
話題例句
環境
Transitioning to sustainable farming practices is essential if the agricultural sector is to meet the food demands of a rapidly growing global population.
若農業部門要滿足快速增長的全球人口的糧食需求，轉向可持續農業做法至關重要。
經濟
Economic growth that relies heavily on the extraction of finite natural resources is, by definition, not sustainable in the long term and will inevitably reach a point of crisis.
嚴重依賴有限自然資源開採的經濟增長，從定義上來說，長期而言是不可持續的，並將不可避免地達到危機點。
pivotal 關鍵的、舉足輕重的

強調某事物在整個系統或過程中居於核心地位，少了它其他事情便無法運作，比 important 或 key 更有分量。

常見搭配 play a pivotal role in prove pivotal in a pivotal moment in
話題例句
教育
Early literacy skills play a pivotal role in determining a child's academic trajectory, with lifelong consequences for employment and social mobility.
早期讀寫能力在決定孩子的學業軌跡上扮演著關鍵角色，對就業和社會流動產生終身影響。
政策
International cooperation has proven pivotal in addressing cross-border environmental challenges that no single nation possesses the capacity to resolve independently.
在應對任何單一國家都無力獨自解決的跨境環境挑戰上，國際合作已被證明舉足輕重。

🟢 描述不均等、邊緣化與公平性

disproportionate 不成比例的

強調某群體承受的負擔或獲得的利益，遠超其在整體中所佔的比例，是指出社會不公平的核心詞彙。

常見搭配 bear a disproportionate burden have a disproportionate impact on disproportionately affect
話題例句
環境
Minority communities bear a disproportionate burden of environmental pollution, as industrial facilities are more frequently located in low-income neighbourhoods.
少數族裔社群承受著不成比例的環境污染負擔，因為工業設施更頻繁地設置在低收入社區。
健康
The disproportionate representation of elderly patients in hospital admissions underscores the urgency of investing in preventive geriatric care.
老年患者在住院人口中不成比例的高度集中，凸顯了投資預防性老年醫療的迫切性。
marginalised 被邊緣化的

描述某群體在社會、政治或經濟上被排除在主流之外，語氣帶有對制度性不公的批判，是社會議題論述的核心詞彙。

常見搭配 marginalised communities marginalised groups increasingly marginalised
話題例句
健康
Marginalised communities are frequently overlooked in the design of public health campaigns, resulting in deeply unequal access to vital health information.
被邊緣化的社群在公共衛生宣傳活動的設計中經常遭到忽視，導致對重要健康資訊的取得嚴重不平等。
教育
Students from marginalised backgrounds are statistically less likely to complete higher education, perpetuating entrenched cycles of social exclusion and economic disadvantage.
來自被邊緣化背景的學生在統計上完成高等教育的可能性較低，延續了根深蒂固的社會排除和經濟弱勢循環。
equitable 公平的、公正的

比 fair 更正式，強調的是「合理的分配」而非「相同的對待」，常用於論述需要差異化政策來達成真正公正的情境。

常見搭配 equitable distribution of an equitable system more equitable access to
話題例句
政策
An equitable distribution of healthcare resources requires targeted investment in underserved communities, rather than uniform national spending that ignores existing disparities.
醫療資源的公平分配，需要對服務不足的社群進行有針對性的投資，而非無視既有差距的全國均一支出。
教育
Designing a truly equitable education system demands more than equal access; it requires dismantling the structural barriers that disadvantage certain groups from the very outset.
設計一個真正公平的教育體系，不僅需要平等的取得機會，還需要從根本上拆除使某些群體從一開始就處於不利地位的結構性障礙。
equitable vs. equal：equal 是「相同的對待」，equitable 是「合理公正的分配」，後者允許差異化措施以達到真正公平，在 Task 2 論述中更具分析深度。

🔷 描述議題的複雜性、爭議性與鮮明對比

contentious 有爭議的、引發爭論的

描述某議題本身即充滿對立觀點，比 controversial 稍更正式，適合用於開場引入議題或承認論點存在合理爭議。

常見搭配 a contentious issue highly contentious remain contentious among
話題例句
政策
Immigration policy remains one of the most contentious issues in contemporary democratic politics, with deeply divided public opinion on both the scale and nature of acceptable migration.
移民政策仍然是當代民主政治中最具爭議性的議題之一，民眾在可接受移民的規模和性質上意見嚴重分歧。
科技
The use of facial recognition technology by law enforcement agencies is highly contentious, raising fundamental concerns about the erosion of privacy and civil liberties.
執法機構使用臉部辨識技術極具爭議，引發了關於隱私和公民自由遭受侵蝕的根本性關切。
nuanced 細緻的、有細微差別的

強調某分析或觀點考慮到了複雜性，而非以非黑即白的方式看待問題，是展現批判性思維的標誌性形容詞。

常見搭配 a nuanced understanding of far more nuanced than a nuanced approach to
話題例句
教育
A nuanced understanding of student performance requires examining not only test scores but also the social, emotional, and economic factors that shape learning outcomes.
對學生表現細緻的理解，需要不僅審視考試成績，還要考量塑造學習結果的社會、情緒和經濟因素。
社會
The relationship between poverty and crime is far more nuanced than popular media representations suggest, involving a complex web of structural, psychological, and historical variables.
貧窮與犯罪之間的關係，遠比大眾媒體的描述更為複雜細緻，涉及結構性、心理性和歷史性變數的複雜交織。
stark 鮮明的（對比）、嚴峻的（現實）

用於突出對比或現實的強烈程度，常搭配 contrast 或 reality，能讓論述的畫面感和衝擊力大幅提升。

常見搭配 a stark contrast between the stark reality of stand in stark contrast to
話題例句
社會
The stark contrast between the living conditions of wealthy and impoverished urban residents highlights the failure of trickle-down economic policies to deliver on their promises.
富裕與貧困城市居民生活條件之間鮮明的對比，突顯了涓滴經濟政策未能兌現承諾的失敗。
環境
The stark reality of melting glaciers and rising sea levels leaves governments with little justification for continued inaction on climate policy.
冰川融化和海平面上升的嚴峻現實，使各國政府幾乎沒有理由繼續在氣候政策上無所作為。
fraught 充滿…的（風險、困難）

強調某過程或情境充滿了難以迴避的問題或風險，幾乎固定搭配 with，語氣帶有警示與複雜性。

常見搭配 fraught with difficulty fraught with risk fraught with ethical concerns
話題例句
政策
The transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy is fraught with logistical and economic challenges that demand careful long-term policy planning.
從化石燃料過渡到可再生能源充滿了後勤和經濟方面的挑戰，需要謹慎的長期政策規劃。
科技
The process of regulating rapidly evolving technologies is fraught with difficulty, as legislation consistently struggles to keep pace with the speed of innovation.
監管快速發展的技術的過程充滿困難，因為立法始終難以跟上創新的速度。

🔵 描述論述的說服力、可察覺性與易感性

compelling 令人信服的、強而有力的

強調某論點或證據有極強的說服力，讓人難以反駁，是 Task 2 論述段和結論段中強化立場的最佳形容詞之一。

常見搭配 a compelling case for compelling evidence that a compelling argument for
話題例句
教育
There is a compelling case for introducing financial literacy education at the secondary school level, given the documented long-term consequences of poor financial decision-making.
鑒於有據可查的財務決策不當的長期後果，在中學階段引入財務素養教育有令人信服的理由。
環境
The scientific consensus on climate change presents a compelling argument for immediate and coordinated global action, regardless of the short-term economic costs involved.
科學界對氣候變遷的共識，為立即採取協調一致的全球行動提供了強而有力的論據，無論涉及何種短期經濟代價。
discernible 可察覺的、明顯的

強調某影響或趨勢雖然存在，但程度上仍有限或剛剛開始顯現，常以否定形式 no discernible 出現，用於批評政策效果不彰。

常見搭配 no discernible improvement in a discernible pattern of barely discernible
話題例句
政策
Despite years of reform efforts, no discernible improvement in educational outcomes has been observed in the lowest-performing school districts.
儘管多年來持續推動改革，在表現最差的學區中，並未觀察到可察覺的教育成果改善。
環境
The long-term effects of microplastic pollution on human health remain uncertain, though some discernible patterns are beginning to emerge from recent longitudinal research.
微塑膠污染對人類健康的長期影響仍不確定，儘管最近的縱向研究開始揭示一些可察覺的規律。
susceptible 易受影響的、易感的

強調某群體或事物特別容易受到某種負面因素的影響，比 vulnerable 更強調「先天或結構性的易感性」。

常見搭配 particularly susceptible to increasingly susceptible to more susceptible to the effects of
話題例句
健康
Elderly individuals are particularly susceptible to the adverse effects of extreme heat, making urban heat island mitigation a critical public health priority.
老年人對極端高溫的不利影響特別易感，使得城市熱島效應的緩解成為重要的公共衛生優先事項。
科技
Young children are especially susceptible to the influence of digital advertising, raising serious ethical concerns about the deliberate targeting of minors by commercial platforms.
幼童對數位廣告的影響特別容易受到影響，引發了關於商業平台刻意針對未成年人行銷的嚴重倫理關切。
susceptible vs. vulnerable：vulnerable 強調「暴露在危險中的脆弱狀態」，susceptible 強調「先天或結構性的易感性」，後者更常用於描述某群體對特定影響的內在敏感度。
