Nelson IELTS — 多層語塊分析

由大到小，層層拆解

同一個句子用三個獨立的語塊分析層次，從骨架到細節，逐層深入。

原始例句
The proliferation of digital streaming platforms, while democratising access to artistic works that were once confined to elite cultural institutions, has simultaneously raised urgent questions about the financial sustainability of independent creators who rely on royalty-based income.
第一層：句子骨架（S + V + O）
The proliferation of digital streaming platforms , while democratising access to artistic works that were once confined to elite cultural institutions, has simultaneously raised urgent questions about the financial sustainability of independent creators who rely on royalty-based income.
第二層：主詞語塊內部結構
The proliferation of digital streaming platforms
第三層：受詞語塊內部結構
urgent questions about the financial sustainability of independent creators who rely on royalty-based income
設計說明

每一個 sentence-stage 是獨立的互動單元，各自的進度條互不干擾。 stage-bridge 提供視覺上的層次過渡，告訴學生「現在要把上一層的某個語塊放大來看」。 由大到小的分析順序符合語塊教學的認知順序：先掌握骨架，再理解細節。
